# About This Book

**Original:** Jane Austen, *Pride and Prejudice* (1813).

**Easy English adaptation:** ChatGPT, with help from Patrick Stein.

**Illustrations:** Hugh Thomson, from the 1894 George Allen edition.

This is the complete novel in accessible, present-day English. It is not a shortened retelling. Every event, conversation, letter, and comment from the narrator remains present. Long sentences have been divided and rare words have been replaced where possible.

This edition aims broadly at accessible B1 English. It does not claim to follow a formally regulated Easy Read standard.

The original English text and historical illustrations are in the public domain. The Easy English adaptation and book-building files are provided under the repository's MIT License.
